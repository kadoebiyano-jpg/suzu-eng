ABOUT THE SUIKINREI

The Suikinrei is a special bell inspired by the delicate tones of the Suikinkutsu, a traditional Japanese garden feature passed down since the Edo period.

Its clear, resonant sound gently reaches the heart, much like the soft notes of a koto or a music box.

Since ancient times, it has been cherished as a symbol believed to ward off misfortune and invite good fortune, and continues to be carefully preserved to this day.
